# US Beauty Ad Compliance Precheck

Flag unsupported cosmetic claims, misleading creative, rights issues and platform policy risks.

## Checks
- Cosmetic/drug intended-use boundary; flag acne treatment, SPF/sunscreen, dandruff treatment, disease and structure/function claims.
- Every objective claim has evidence and appropriate qualification.
- Detect absolute promises, guaranteed results, unsupported clinical/dermatologist claims, misleading before/after, fake testimonials and altered outcome depictions.
- Check ad-to-landing-page consistency for product, price, offer and terms.
- Check trademark, music, font, model, likeness and footage rights.
- Check current market/platform requirements and AIGC disclosure.

## Decisions
PASS, PASS_WITH_CHANGES, BLOCK, NEEDS_REGULATORY_REVIEW.

This precheck is not legal advice and does not guarantee platform approval.
