# Beauty Ad Creative Strategy

Create differentiated, testable concepts for US beauty/skincare.

## Inputs
Approved product facts, evidence-backed claims, brand voice, audience hypothesis, channel, format, objective.

## Rules
- Map every objective product claim to claim_id and evidence status.
- Specify hook, context, demonstration, proof type, CTA, format, length and test variable.
- Do not invent testimonials, clinical results, dermatologist endorsements, ingredient efficacy or before/after outcomes.
- Keep price, offer and landing-page facts consistent.
- Audience hypotheses must not rely on sensitive health attributes.

## Output
creative_matrix[]: concept_id, hypothesis, angle, hook, narrative, CTA, claims_used[], risks[], test_variable.
